Privacy & Support

Last updated 27 August 2026

The short version: this app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, anywhere, ever.

Privacy Policy

Accounts

No sign-in, no account

GastroPadel Score has no account system. There's nothing to register for and nothing tied to your identity.

Match data

Stays on your device

Scores, team names, and settings are held only in the app's local storage on your phone. None of it is sent to a server, because the app doesn't have one. Uninstalling the app removes this data completely.

Tracking

No analytics, no advertising

The app includes no analytics SDKs, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party trackers of any kind. Your use of the app is not measured or reported to anyone, including us.

Clicker

A local Bluetooth connection only

If you pair a Clicker, the app talks to it directly over Bluetooth to register button presses. This connection is local to your device and involves no data leaving your phone.

Changes

If this ever changes

Should a future version of the app introduce any data collection, this policy will be updated first, with the change described plainly here.

Support

Contact

Get in touch

Questions, feedback, or something not working as expected? Email privacy@gastropadel.es and we'll get back to you.
